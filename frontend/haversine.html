Haversine Formula

Computes the great-circle distance between two points on a sphere given their latitude and longitude.

Formula

a = sin²(Δφ/2) + cos(φ₁) · cos(φ₂) · sin²(Δλ/2)
c = 2 · atan2(√a, √(1−a))
d = R · c

Where φ = latitude, λ = longitude, R = Earth's radius (6,371 km).

Calculator

Defaults: Mumbai → New Delhi. Enter values in decimal degrees (negative = South / West).

Notes

  • Assumes a spherical Earth (radius 6,371 km). For higher accuracy use the Vincenty or Karney formulas on an ellipsoid.
  • Maximum error ≈ 0.3% compared to geodesic distance on the WGS-84 ellipsoid.
  • Preferred over the spherical law of cosines for small distances due to better numerical stability.
